# Independent critique of Plan A's frozen claim list (optional part of the 20t brief; 2026-11-01)
Written by the Plan B feasibility session from PLANS.md and RESULTS_LEDGER.md only. No new analysis was run; every
number below is quoted from the cited ledger entry. This is a reviewer's reading, not a decision.

| Claim | What the ledger supports | Where the wording goes further than that |
|---|---|---|
| C1 "Wake contains two states, Q and W" | 20b theta sensitivity: the DIRECTION of the cycle is unchanged at theta 0.33/0.5/0.75; magnitudes grow with theta. | The theta check shows the cycle survives the threshold, not that wake has two STATES. A threshold on activity fraction splits any continuum. "Two states" needs bimodality (or a better 2- vs 1-component fit) of per-minute wake activity; nothing in the cited entries tests that. Safer: "splitting wake at theta x r_f reveals a directed cycle robust to theta". |
| C4 "HMM value as upper bracket (J x1.0-1.2, EPR x1.2-3.6 per fly)" | 20s-A per-fly HMM/obs medians. | Not an upper bracket per fly or always at the median. Per-fly IQRs go below 1 (CS light J 0.23-1.08; sss dark EPR 0.55-2.67). Model EPR medians are BELOW the observed chain in CS light (0.032 vs 0.037), fumin light (0.160 vs 0.163) and sss light (0.008 vs 0.046). The post hoc per-fly check finds HMM EPR >= the triplet bound in only 44-73% of fly-phases. Safer: "the HMM's values are typically larger (median x1.2-3.6)". |
| C5 "Onsager L higher in per0 DD than CS (p 0.026)" | 20s-A addendum 2, fly-bootstrap differences for five genotype comparisons. | One of 5 comparisons, uncorrected. With BH across the five, per0DD q = 0.065 (sss q = 0.003 survives). Also a condition confound: per0 DD (one whole DD record) is compared with CS in LD (both phases pooled). The like-for-like contrast, per0 DD vs CS DD (L 0.352 vs 0.262), is not in the ledger as a test. |
| C5 "faster than linearly ... (CS, per0LD, sss)" | 20s-A A2. | sss is clear on count points (bootstrap share linear 0.06) but ambiguous on exact-chain points (0.34). Physically, EPR ~ J^2/L is the near-equilibrium expectation for a single cycle, so the quadratic is expected, not a discovery. |
| C3 "the circulation switches on at night" | 20s-A C3: night minus day c_obs +0.12 (80% of flies, p 0.0004). | "Switches on" implies off by day. ZT6-12 is already forward (c_obs +0.043 [+0.013, +0.101]; count J +0.125 [+0.042, +0.208]). Safer: "strengthens at night" / "is mainly nocturnal". |
| C9 "extra irreversibility only in per0 DD" | 20p qualifying rule. | fumin dark also qualifies (E +0.040, Holm 0.0002). PLANS line 3 states it, but the claim drops it. Its sleep-criterion confound is open; that is a reason to caveat it, not to omit it. |
| C9 "cycle sits at the TUR bound" | 20q: Q_MC 0.90-1.00 (the fitted chain at its own bound); data Q_obs 0.90 per0 DD, 0.75 CS DD, 0.51-0.95 LD. | Q_MC near 1 is a property of near-equilibrium unicyclic chains (20q amendment C says so), not a finding about flies. The DATA reach the bound only in per0 DD (0.90) and CS dark (0.95). Safer: "no TUR evidence for hidden dissipation; the observed current is at most as precise as the observed chain allows". |

Claims C2, C6, C7 and C8 read as consistent with their entries, given the caveats already written into them (C8: one
cohort each, bin-sensitive). C8's support sits on another branch (33736f1) and was not re-read here.
