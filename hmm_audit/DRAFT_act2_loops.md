## Sentence (Sheyum, 2026-11-03)
Removing the clock does not make the cycle's direction less steady: loop by loop, *per0* flies switch direction as
often as wild type, and in both the sequence of directions is that of a fly tossing its own biased coin (about 60:40
forward), with no episodes of backward running (Fig. S12a,b).

## Optional second sentence (for Results, if space)
Same-direction runs of loops were no longer than each fly's own Markov chain predicts, in clockless or wild-type flies
(per0 DD vs CS DD, p ≈ 0.3–0.4), so the minute-scale memory we detect elsewhere (Figs. ...) does not add up to switching of
the drive over hours.

## Wording rules
- "as often as wild type" rests on the pre-registered per0-vs-CS comparison of the chain-corrected excess
  (per0 DD vs CS DD p ≈ 0.3–0.4; per0 LD vs CS LD p ≈ 0.6; quote ranges, because the simulation spread alone moves these p by ~0.08, lClaude 2026-11-04). It is a null comparison: say "did not differ", never "identical".
- "biased coin" = independent loop directions given the fly's chain. The observed same-direction fraction is close to a
  shuffled order (per0 DD 0.526 vs 0.528).
- Do not quote the per0 DD clock-null p 0.034 (Holm 0.068) as a trend.
